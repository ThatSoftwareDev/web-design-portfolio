# Designs: Landing Page Concepts

`landing-pages.pdf` contains full-page concept designs for three small businesses.

| Page | Industry | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| Tierheim Wiesengrund | Animal shelter, Hamburg | Warm green and cream color palette, serif headlines with italic accent |
| Tischlerei Voss | Carpentry, Hamburg | Warm wood tones, off-white and charcoal, serif headlines |
| SCHWARZ&GOLD | Hair studio, Berlin | Elegant black and gold, high-contrast serif, menu-style price list |

Language: German.
